Purpose:
Generate necessary Amber topology files, perform partial decomposition analysis calculations on QM/MM trajectories, and collect and organize residue decomposition results.

The order of scripts to use for this workflow is noted below.

1. generate_ligand_enzyme_complex_parm.sh
2. prepare_and_queue_all_pda_jobs.sh
3. sort_pda_results.sh

All other included scripts are executed within these and do not need to be executed on their own.
